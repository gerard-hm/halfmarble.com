StringFusor — Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 29, 2026

StringFusor is a game published by Halfmarble LLC (“halfmarble”, “we”, “us”). This policy explains how StringFusor handles your information.

The short version: StringFusor does not collect any data about you. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking.

Data we collect

None. StringFusor does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data, and it does not track you across other apps or websites. There is no advertising identifier (IDFA) and no attribution.

Information stored on your device

Your game settings, progress, replays, and best scores are stored locally on your device by iOS. This information never leaves your device through StringFusor, and it is removed when you delete the app.

Game Center

If you choose to use Game Center, your leaderboard scores are submitted to Apple’s Game Center service and handled by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy. StringFusor does not collect, store, or receive this information itself, and using Game Center is optional. To keep scores private, you can turn Game Center off in your device Settings.

Purchases

StringFusor offers one optional in-app purchase. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store; StringFusor never receives your payment information.

Third parties

StringFusor contains no third-party SDKs — no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or social frameworks. No third-party partners receive any data from the app.

Children

StringFusor does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children. Because we collect no data at all, no personal information from children (or adults) is gathered.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
